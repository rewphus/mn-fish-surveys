Lake Fish Surveys, 2025–2026

Every species logged in a Minnesota DNR lake survey dated 2025 or 2026 — catch counts, CPUE, average weight, and measured size range, pulled directly from DNR's own survey API.

Most-encountered species (by number of lakes)

Coverage: built from a candidate list of 959 lakes tracked in DNR's biotic-integrity assessment layer, cross-checked against each lake's actual survey history — not a complete census of every 2025–2026 MN fish survey. That layer systematically excludes DNR's Large Lake Program waters (confirmed via Mille Lacs, which has a real 2025 survey but wasn't in the candidate list at all), so 8 of Minnesota's biggest lakes — Kabetogama, Lake of the Woods, Leech, Mille Lacs, Rainy, Upper Red, Vermilion, and Winnibigoshish — were added by hand after being individually verified against DNR's API. Lake Pepin (the Mississippi River pool one) is still missing; its DOW number couldn't be reliably located and it may not follow the standard lake-survey pattern at all. DNR runs roughly 650 surveys/year statewide; this dataset found 524 across 387 lakes. Of those, 77 surveys (mostly "Targeted Survey" type — likely invasive-species or single-species checks) logged no fish catch at all, which is why the table below reflects catch data for fewer lakes than 387. Species with zero catch in a given lake/year simply don't appear as rows.

Source: Minnesota DNR LakeFinder survey API (maps.dnr.state.mn.us) and DNR Fisheries species reference data. Not an official DNR product.
